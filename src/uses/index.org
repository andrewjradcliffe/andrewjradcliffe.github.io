What I reach for. Subject to change; the good tools rarely do.

* Editor & OS
- *Emacs.* Not an editor so much as a Lisp machine that happens to edit
  text. This site is built inside it. Org mode is the substrate for
  notes, drafts, and — evidently — entire websites.
- *Linux.* The whole environment is a programmable surface. The shell
  is the IDE; the man pages are the docs; =/proc= is the truth.

* Languages
- *Rust* for anything that has to be correct and fast at the same time.
  The borrow checker is a colleague who is annoying and always right.
- *Julia* for numerical work and prototyping — where I want
  mathematical notation to survive contact with the keyboard.
- *Python* when the ecosystem leaves no choice. A complicated language
  wearing a simple one's clothes; respect it and it mostly behaves.

* Command-line tooling
- =ripgrep= for search; =fd= for find; =xan= for pushing CSV/XLSX around
  without summoning a 15-gigabyte interpreter to do it.
- =git=, obviously. =make= for anything that runs more than twice.
- =awk= and the rest of the Unix workshop, for the jobs too small to
  deserve a program and too annoying to do by hand.
