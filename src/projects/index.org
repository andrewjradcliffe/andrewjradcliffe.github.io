* Numerically-stable evaluation
The unglamorous arithmetic that keeps everything downstream finite.

** lnexp
=crates.io: v0.2.1= · =MIT OR Apache-2.0= · [[https://crates.io/crates/lnexp][crates.io]] · [[https://github.com/andrewjradcliffe/lnexp][source]]

Compositions of =log=, =log1p=, =exp=, and =expm1=, carefully
evaluated so as to avoid underflow/overflow.

** logsumexp
=crates.io: v0.1.0= · =MIT OR Apache-2.0= · [[https://crates.io/crates/logsumexp][crates.io]] · [[https://github.com/andrewjradcliffe/logsumexp][source]]

Online (1-pass) [[https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LogSumExp][LogSumExp]] for iterators.

** horner-eval
=crates.io: v0.1.0= · =MIT OR Apache-2.0= · [[https://crates.io/crates/horner-eval][crates.io]] · [[https://github.com/andrewjradcliffe/horner-eval][source]]

Evaluate polynomials using [[https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Horner%27s_method][Horner's method]]. Unroll polynomials of
fixed size at compile time (and use =MulAdd=); useful if writing
numerical libraries.

** LogExpFunctionsExt.jl
=Julia= · =MIT= · =merged upstream= → [[https://github.com/JuliaStats/LogExpFunctions.jl][LogExpFunctions.jl]]

Merged into the [[https://github.com/JuliaStats/LogExpFunctions.jl][LogExpFunctions.jl]] package by way of [[https://github.com/JuliaStats/LogExpFunctions.jl/pull/82][PR #82]];
no longer a standalone package, which is the happiest fate a small
extension can have.

=Julia: v0.1.0= · =MIT= · [[https://github.com/andrewjradcliffe/CategoricalMonteCarlo.jl][source]]

transformation.

** Whitening.jl
=Julia: v0.1.2= · =MIT= · [[https://github.com/andrewjradcliffe/Whitening.jl][source]]

** OptimizationTestFunctions.jl
=Julia: v0.1.0= · =MIT= · [[https://github.com/andrewjradcliffe/OptimizationTestFunctions.jl][source]]

A wide variety of functions, $f : \mathbb{R}^{n} \mapsto \mathbb{R}$,

* Data-wrangling & toys
** RecursiveTiles.jl
=Julia: v0.1.1= · =MIT= · [[https://github.com/andrewjradcliffe/RecursiveTiles.jl][source]]

Ever receive the following? A table comprised of flattened trees,
with:
- parent-child relationships spanning more than one column
- arbitrary order of columns, with no correspondence to parent-child
  relationship
- multiple flattened trees vertically concatenated into the same
  table, but with unspecified begin/end
If so, welcome to my life a few years ago. This package will enable
you to roll that mess back into trees with a few lines of code.
