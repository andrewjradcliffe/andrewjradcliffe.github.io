I am Andrew Radcliffe. I work on numerical methods, Monte Carlo, and
the machine-learning systems that lean on them — mostly in Rust and
Julia, occasionally in whatever the problem demands. I like small,
correct tools and proofs that fit in the head.

This site collects the open-source work, the occasional pre-print, and
a blog where I think out loud. None of it is finished; see the [[/blog/2026-06-01-imagine-the-draft-happy/][first post]]
for why I consider that a feature.
